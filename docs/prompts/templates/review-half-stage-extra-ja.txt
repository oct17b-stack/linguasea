- Text: stages A–D are written with spaces between phrases (わかちがき), E–F without; kanji only for words the course has already taught in kanji, kana otherwise (also in readers). Years and counters: decide one consistent way (e.g. 〜年 in kanji only after 年 has been taught in kanji) and record it.
- Thai transliteration: word-final せん → เซ็น, えん → เอ็น (never เซ็ง/เอ็ง); 円 → เยน; …める/…れる endings must not spell เมรุ (crematorium): write เมรู etc. consistently.
- Romaji: Hepburn with macrons, particles wa/e/o, spaces between words.
