
## Japanese-specific
- Cast: ナム Namu = Nam (female, says わたし); たなか ゆき Tanaka Yuki (female friend, `c`); やまだせんせい Yamada sensei (male teacher, `b`). In A–B everyone speaks polite です/ます style (Nam and Yuki switch to plain style only from C7).
- Thai transliteration: word-final せん → เซ็น and えん → เอ็น (never เซ็ง/เอ็ง); 円 → เยน; make る-endings (…める, …べる) and long vowels consistent across the stages.
- Romaji: Hepburn with macrons (arigatō, sū), particles は/へ/を written wa/e/o, spaces between words.
- Kana drills: the options of a listening question must sound different.
