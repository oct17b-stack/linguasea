
## Korean-specific
- Cast: 남 Nam (female; calls an older female friend 언니 and an older male friend 오빠); 김민준 Gim Minjun (male friend, `b`); 이 선생님 (female teacher, `c`). Stages A–B: present tense only and polite 해요체 everywhere (Nam and Minjun switch to 반말 only from ko-E3-01).
- Readings: Revised Romanization of the actual pronunciation (linking, nasalization, tensing, aspiration), consistent across the course.
- Hangul drills: the options of a listening question must be audibly different in standard Seoul Korean; ㅐ/ㅔ and ㅚ/ㅙ/ㅞ must never be listening alternatives; `say` values must produce the intended sound.
- Thai transliteration: follow the conventions in content/_review/ko.md; where the Royal Society's Korean transcription looks bizarre to ordinary Thai readers for complex syllables, prefer the simpler common spelling, consistently.
