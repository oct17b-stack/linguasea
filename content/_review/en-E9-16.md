## 写法与读音

- 大纲词条的读音照抄，句子里按全课约定读：例如 be known for 的词条读音是 bi ˈnoʊn fɔːr，句子里的 for 弱读成 fər；Do you need a hand? 词条是 duː juː ˌniːd ə ˈhænd，I'll miss you 是 aɪl ˈmɪs juː。
- must 的读音：表示推测时不重读（[en-E16-01] It must be the wind.、[en-E16-03] It must be a drone show. 写 mʌst）；[en-E10-03] You must try khao soi! 这类强烈推荐写重读 ˈmʌst。[en-E16-04] must have / might have 的 have 按约定写完整形式 hæv，讲解另外说明口语里常读 əv（must've），不能写成 must of。请确认。
- into 按全课写法一律写 ˈɪntuː（[en-E15-06] move into the dormitory 等）。
- 不是词条的名词+名词组合（English test、visa forms、visa decisions）按普通短语写两个主重音，和 [en-E3-r02] 的 English test certificate 一致。
- [en-E12-04] I wish it were Friday today!：照大纲用 were，讲解说明口语也可以用 was。
- [en-E13-04] Maybe by then you won't want it any more.、[en-E9-06]、[en-E10-04]、[en-E14-r02]：拼成 any more，和 [en-D7-06] 的词条一致（美式更常拼 anymore）。
- [en-E14-03] Where shall I put them?：美式口语少用 shall，[en-E14-03] 的讲解说 Shall I…? 前面学过，所以保留；要不要改成 Where should I put them?
- [en-E14-01] That's great experience.、[en-E13-r02] Now the week is over, and I didn't buy anything new!（美式用一般过去时）、[en-E13-05] Too much knowledge, too much stuff.（玩笑话）：请英语老师看是否自然。
- [en-E13-05] gasoline 照大纲；讲解说明美国人口语常说 gas。
- 日期一律写序数形式：June 5th、June 26th、February 20th、August 20th。
- [en-E16-05] She also told me to wear pink every Tuesday、She wears green every Wednesday：every + 单数星期。
