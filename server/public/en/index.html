Who it’s for

Made for Thai speakers starting a new language, whether they’re still studying or already working. All explanations are in Thai; versions for more Southeast Asian languages, starting with Vietnamese and Indonesian, are planned.

  • Complete beginners

    No background needed. Start with greetings, sounds and the writing system, then build up step by step.

  • Students

    Build solid foundations aligned with HSK, JLPT, TOPIK and CEFR levels before further study or a real exam.

  • Working adults

    Greet people, introduce yourself and handle simple conversations with customers and colleagues from abroad.

  • Travelers and fans

    Order food and ask prices and directions on your own when you travel, and start catching simple words in the shows and songs you love.

What’s in a lesson

Each lesson takes about 6–11 minutes, easy to finish on the bus or during a lunch break.

  1. New words

    See each word with its romanization, Thai pronunciation guide and meaning. Listen at normal or slow speed.

  2. Dialogue

    Meet the new words in a short conversation, with a Thai translation of every line.

  3. Good to know

    Quick notes on grammar, pronunciation and culture, explained in Thai and compared with Thai.

  4. Listening and reading

    Listen and choose the meaning, read a word and choose its translation, or choose the word you heard.

  5. Speaking

    Listen, repeat out loud and see your pronunciation score right away.

  6. Earn XP

    Finish a lesson to earn XP and keep your streak of learning days going.

Review before you forget

Words you’ve learned come back for review on the right day, scheduled by spaced repetition, so they stick without daily cramming.

Practice sessions, graded stories and unit tests

Every two lessons there is a practice session, every unit ends with a test, and from Level B on there are graded stories to read with comprehension questions. Studied before? Take the placement test and skip what you already know.

Speak for real, not just memorize

Two tools that build your confidence to speak, in Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean.

AI conversation partner

After each unit, try a real conversation in that unit’s situation, such as introducing yourself, ordering a drink or asking a price. The AI plays the other person and sticks mostly to words you’ve learned. Type or speak your answers.

  • Every line comes with romanization and a Thai translation
  • Mistakes are explained kindly in Thai, with the corrected sentence
  • Stuck? A hint suggests what to say next

Your partner is an AI, not a real person. There’s no need to share personal details such as your full name, phone number or address.

Pronunciation scoring

Repeat after the app and get your result instantly: a score from 0 to 100, plus word-by-word feedback showing what you said well and what to practice.

  • Works with the words and phrases in each lesson
  • See exactly what to fix instead of guessing

Free users can try the AI partner and pronunciation scoring a few times a day; Premium members can use them more each day.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? What does Premium add?

Yes, you can learn for free. Level A (Beginner) is free in all four languages, every lesson of it, along with practice sessions, unit tests, vocabulary review, XP and streaks. Free users can also try the AI conversation partner and pronunciation scoring a few times a day.

Premium unlocks Levels B to F (Basic to Upper intermediate) in every language, including the graded stories, and lets you use the AI partner and pronunciation scoring more each day. The app always shows the price before you confirm.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial (if your store account hasn’t used one before). If you don’t want to continue, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won’t be charged.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. If you subscribed in the app, cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. If you subscribed on the web, cancel with the manage-subscription link in your receipt email. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you’ve already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Step-by-step instructions for iPhone and Android

How many levels are there, and how far do they go?

Every language has six levels (A–F) of 16 units each, about 200 hours of study. Level A is free; Levels B–F are part of Premium.

After Level F the content is comparable to HSK 4 (Chinese), JLPT N3 (Japanese), TOPIK II level 3 (Korean) and CEFR B1 (English). See the level table

Do I need any background?

No. Level A starts from greetings, sounds and the writing system, and every word has a pronunciation guide in Thai script. You do need to read Thai, because all explanations are in Thai.

I’ve studied before. Do I have to start from zero?

No. When you start a language, choose “I know a little” (พอรู้มาบ้าง) and take a short placement test, one level at a time. Each level you pass is marked as done, so you start at the next one. You can retake the test later in Settings.

Can I learn more than one language?

Yes. Switch languages anytime in the app without losing the lessons you’ve finished, and one Premium subscription covers all four.
