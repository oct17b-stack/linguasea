Contact us

Email us at [TBD: support email] and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can. You can write in Thai or English.

To help us help you faster, please include:

  • Your phone model and system version (on iPhone: Settings → General → About; on Android: Settings → About phone)
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the app’s ตั้งค่า (Settings) screen
  • The language you’re learning and the lesson where the problem happened
  • What happened, with a screenshot if you can
  • For payment questions, the order ID from your Apple or Google receipt, or, if you subscribed on the web, the email address you signed in with

We will never ask for your password or card details. Please don’t send them by email.

Sign in with email

You can learn without signing in. If you sign in with your email address, your progress and subscription belong to your account: you can use them on every device where you sign in with the same email, including on the web, and they aren’t lost when you change phones or clear your browser data. To subscribe on the web, you need to sign in first.

  1. Open the app or the web version and tap the gear icon (ตั้งค่า, Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Under บัญชี (Account), enter your email address and tap ส่งรหัสเข้าสู่ระบบ (Send sign-in code).
  3. Open the email, enter the 6-digit code within 10 minutes and tap ยืนยัน (Confirm).

Good to know

  • There is no password. Each time you sign in on a new device, we email you a new code. Can’t see the email? Check your spam folder, or tap ส่งรหัสใหม่ (Send a new code).
  • If you studied on a device before signing in, that progress is merged into your account, not lost.
  • You can sign out under บัญชี (Account). Your other signed-in devices stay signed in.

Restore purchases

Subscribed to Premium, but the app isn’t unlocked, for example after switching phones or reinstalling the app?

If you subscribed on the web, or you had signed in with your email, there is nothing to restore: just sign in with the same email address and Premium comes back. If you subscribed on the App Store or Google Play, restore your purchase:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you subscribed with.
  2. Open the app and tap the gear icon (ตั้งค่า, Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  3. Under การสมัครสมาชิก (Subscription), tap กู้คืนการซื้อ (Restore purchases).
  4. Wait a moment. If your subscription is still active, Premium comes back right away.

Subscriptions bought on the App Store can be restored on iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account. Subscriptions bought on Google Play can be restored on Android devices with the same Google account.

Still not working? Check that the subscription hasn’t expired (you can see it in the store’s subscription settings, as described under Cancel your subscription), connect to the internet, update the app to the latest version and try again. If it still doesn’t work, contact us with your order ID or the email address you signed in with.

Cancel your subscription

Cancel where you subscribed: if you subscribed in the app, cancel in the App Store or Google Play account you subscribed with; if you subscribed on the web, cancel with the link in your receipt email. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap our app’s subscription.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription. You might need to scroll down to find it.

If there’s no Cancel button, or you see an expiration message in red, the subscription is already canceled.

Shortcut: open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad · Apple’s instructions

Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Select our app’s subscription.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions.

Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions in a browser and sign in with the Google account you subscribed with · Google’s instructions

Subscribed on the web

  1. Open the receipt email you got when you subscribed (sent through our payment providers, RevenueCat and Stripe).
  2. Tap the link to manage your subscription.
  3. Cancel on the page that opens.

Web subscriptions can’t be canceled in the App Store or Google Play. Can’t find the receipt email? Contact us from the email address you signed in with and we’ll help you cancel.

Good to know

  • After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.
  • On a free trial and don’t want to continue? Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends so you won’t be charged (on the web, cancelling before the trial ends is enough).
  • For store subscriptions, refunds follow the store’s policies. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple’s instructions). On Android, see how to request a refund on Google Play, or contact us.
  • For web subscriptions, we handle refunds ourselves: contact us from the email address you signed in with (see section 4.5 of the Terms of Use).

Delete your data

You can delete your account and the learning data stored with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app or the web version and tap the gear icon (ตั้งค่า, Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Under ข้อมูลของฉัน (My data), tap ลบข้อมูลของฉัน (Delete my data).
  3. Tap ลบ (Delete) to confirm.

This deletes all your progress, XP and learning stats, including the data stored on our server. If you signed in with email, it deletes the whole account, including your email address, and signs out every device.

Already deleted the app, or can’t open it?

Send a request on our account and data deletion page.

  • Deleted data, including your learning progress, can’t be recovered.
  • Deleting your data does not cancel a Premium subscription. To stop it from renewing, also cancel your subscription. If you subscribed in a store and want to keep Premium, tap กู้คืนการซื้อ (Restore purchases) afterwards. A web subscription belongs to the deleted account; if you still want to use it, please contact us.

To see what we collect and how long we keep it, read our Privacy Policy.

Audio and microphone

No sound

  • Turn up the volume and make sure silent mode is off.
  • If Bluetooth headphones are connected, check that the sound is going to the device you want, or disconnect them.
  • Use a stable internet connection, because lesson audio is loaded from the internet.
  • If the voice sounds like the wrong accent, or there’s no voice at all, install a voice for that language on your device. On iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. On Android, search for “Text-to-speech” in the Settings app.
  • Close and reopen the app, or restart your phone.

Microphone not working, or no score after speaking

  • Allow the app to use the microphone. On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on our app. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → our app → Permissions → Microphone and choose Allow only while using the app. Menu names can differ slightly between phone brands.
  • Close other apps that use the microphone, such as phone calls, video calls or voice recorders.
  • Speak in a quiet place, hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, and start speaking once recording has started.
  • Say the whole word or phrase. If the app says it didn’t hear anything, speak a little louder and more clearly.
  • If recordings through Bluetooth headphones sound unclear, disconnect them and speak into the phone’s own microphone.
  • Pronunciation scoring and the AI partner need an internet connection. If the app says you’ve reached today’s limit, try again tomorrow.

Still having trouble? Update the app to the latest version, then contact us with your phone model and system version.
